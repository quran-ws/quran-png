How to put Quran text in a Canva design

Canva has no Arabic Quran text that renders correctly — pasting Uthmani script into a text box breaks the diacritics, and most "Quran fonts" are not the mushaf. The reliable way is to place the verse as an image.

1. Pick the ayahs

Choose the surah and the range above. The preview is the real artwork, at the real proportions.

2. Choose a layout

Mushaf lines keeps the line breaks exactly as they fall on the printed page — the truest reproduction. Fit to width repacks the same words into a square, a 4:5 post, a 9:16 story or a banner, which usually sits better in a social layout. Neither option ever stretches or distorts a letter.

3. Download and upload

Download the PNG and drag it into Canva's Uploads panel. It is transparent, so it sits on any background. On Canva Pro, upload the SVG instead — it stays sharp at any size and you can recolour it inside Canva. For print, use the PDF.

Or skip the upload

The quran-canva app puts the same picker inside the Canva editor, so you can add an ayah to your design without leaving the page.

Why this is the printed mushaf, not a font

Every word here is vector artwork traced from the King Fahd Complex's own Madani mushaf plates — 77,432 words and 436,398 individual diacritical marks, each one a separate shape. Nothing is typeset at request time, so there is no font to go missing, no shaping engine to get the ligatures wrong, and no difference between what you see and what the mushaf prints.
