Not a Quran-like font

The Quran is not typeset again every time you export.

Each word and diacritic is stored as vector artwork and used directly to produce the PNG, SVG, or PDF.

No font dependency. No shaping engine rebuilding the words. The artwork you preview is the artwork you export.
