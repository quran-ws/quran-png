# Notice

## The Qur'anic text and the mushaf artwork

Every image this service produces is cut from the **King Fahd Glorious Qur'an
Printing Complex**'s Muṣḥaf al-Madīnah, V4 (1441H), in the riwāyah of Ḥafṣ ʿan
ʿĀṣim. The artwork and the text are the Complex's.

The Complex publishes a complete free digital copy of the muṣḥaf — Illustrator
files, PDF, high-quality images and a TrueType font — and states its terms
directly. Based on the approval of the Minister of Islamic Affairs, Daʿwah and
Guidance, General Supervisor of the Complex:

> These formats can be used for free in all personal and individual businesses,
> in works of governmental departments & agencies, in the publications of both
> private and national institutions; it also allows Qur'an printing\*, digital
> publishing, media use, and use in websites, software, and other similar
> intermediates.
>
> \* The printing of the Qur'an inside the Kingdom and importing it from abroad
> for the purpose of commercial sale is subject to Royal Decree No. 136/8
> (1/2/1406 AH) and Royal Decree No. 9/B/46356 (28/9/1424 AH), which prohibit
> printing the Qur'an inside the Kingdom anywhere other than the King Fahd
> Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex, and prohibit importing it from abroad for the
> purpose of commercial sale.

Source: <https://qurancomplex.gov.sa>.

In practice that is free for personal, commercial, governmental, digital, media,
web and software use, worldwide. The one reserved right is physical: the
print-for-commercial-sale muṣḥaf trade, which belongs to the Complex. Nothing in
this repository touches it.

Attribution is not a condition, but it is right: the site credits the Complex on
every page, and so should anything built on this API.

## The decomposition

The vector decomposition, the shape labels and the word indexes that make an
individual ayah addressable come from
[`quran-svg-pipeline`](https://github.com/AbdullahObaid/quran-svg-pipeline),
released under **CC BY 4.0**, with attribution waived for use inside a product.

## Adab

These are images of the Qur'an. The service renders the text and nothing else:
no translation, no commentary, no alteration, and no partial word. A range is
either drawn in full or refused. Please handle the output accordingly.
